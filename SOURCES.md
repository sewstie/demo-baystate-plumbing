Massachusetts outline: MassGIS municipal boundaries dissolved into a state outline, simplified to 0.003 degrees for display. Includes Cape Cod and the islands.
https://services1.arcgis.com/7iJyYTjCtKsZS1LR/arcgis/rest/services/MA_outline/FeatureServer/3

Worcester County outline: Plotly public county GeoJSON, FIPS 25027.
https://github.com/plotly/datasets/blob/master/geojson-counties-fips.json

Town markers: MassGIS Town and City Halls. Points mark municipal offices, not service coverage boundaries. All geometry uses the same equirectangular projection with longitude scaled by cos(42.2°), fitted separately to each view.
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-town-and-city-halls
https://services1.arcgis.com/hGdibHYSPO59RG1h/ArcGIS/rest/services/Town_and_City_Halls/FeatureServer/0
